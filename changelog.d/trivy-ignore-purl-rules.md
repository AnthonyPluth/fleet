The accepted image findings' check (`trivy-image-ignore-file`, `.github/scripts/trivy_ignore.py`) holds a purl to the rules Trivy v0.70.0's purl parser (`packageurl-go` v0.1.5) has for five types, and says which rule a refused purl broke instead of describing a form the purl already had: `cpan`, `swift` and `vscode-extension` need a namespace (`cpan`'s in capitals), `otp` takes none, and `julia` needs a `uuid` qualifier, so no `julia` purl is accepted. Those five types were refused outright before; the forms the parser takes (`pkg:cpan/AUTHOR/Name`, `pkg:otp/name`, `pkg:swift/github.com/owner/name`, `pkg:vscode-extension/publisher/name`) are now accepted. `cran` and `conan` purls stay accepted as they were: that parser has no rule for either (earlier `packageurl-go` releases did). The Self-test's `accepted-finding` job again hands the real Trivy a path-only entry as well as one with `purls`, and the latter now carries every form the check accepts of every type the parser knows (`tests/fixtures/security/purls.txt`: with and without a namespace, with and without a version, so the versionless `pkg:swift/github.com/owner/name` form too; a test holds the fixture to exactly those forms), so a Trivy that refuses one fails fleet's Self-test, not an app's image scan. The job has a control as well: a list holding one purl the parser refuses (`pkg:swift/name`), on an entry that matches no finding, must fail its scan and for that purl, so a pass shows Trivy parsed the fixture's purls rather than skipped them. A test fails when `TRIVY_VERSION` moves without the rules being read again from the new Trivy's parser (`PURL_RULES_FROM`; `docs/adopting.md` says how). What apps do: nothing.
